    Learn

    RSI — Relative Strength Index

    RSI measures how fast and how far a price has moved recently, on a 0–100 scale. Above 70 is often called “overbought”, below 30 “oversold” — but in a strong trend RSI can stay extreme for a long time, so it is a clue, not a signal by itself.

    Moving-average crossover

    When a fast moving average (e.g. SMA 50) crosses above a slow one (e.g. SMA 200) — the “golden cross” — it suggests momentum has turned up; the reverse (“death cross”) suggests the opposite. Crossovers lag the market: they confirm trends late and whipsaw in sideways markets.

    Risk management

    Decide before you trade: how much of your capital one trade may risk (many use 1–2%), where your stop-loss sits, and how large the position is. Surviving losing streaks matters more than any single winning trade.

    Why backtests can mislead

    Look-ahead bias (using tomorrow's data today), ignoring commissions and slippage, over-fitting rules to one lucky period, and survivorship bias can all make a weak strategy look brilliant. Honest backtests use next-bar fills, charge real costs, and still only describe the past.

    About AlgoTrade AI

    AlgoTrade AI is a learning-first platform for algorithmic trading. It brings together a visual strategy builder, an honest backtesting engine, a marketplace of example strategies and a paper-trading simulator — so you can take an idea like “what happens if I buy when the fast moving average crosses above the slow one?” and turn it into a tested, practised result, all in one place.

    It is built for complete beginners and experienced traders alike: no code is needed to create your first strategy, while advanced users can write Python strategies in the full version. The full version works with delayed market data that is always clearly labelled, with labelled sample data as a fallback. Above all, nothing here trades real money — AlgoTrade AI exists so you can learn how strategies behave, and what they cost, before you ever consider risking a single rupee of your own.
